## Not shipped (fetched on use)

- **Music.** `assets/music/catalog.json` lists Mixkit tracks with their download URL and sha256; `scripts/pick_music.py` downloads a track from Mixkit on first use into `~/.cache/promo-video/music/` and checks it. The Mixkit Stock Music Free License allows use in videos but not standalone redistribution, so no audio file is committed here. It does not cover broadcast TV/radio or games.
- **Fonts.** `assets/fonts/catalog.json` is a catalogue; `scripts/fonts.py fetch` downloads each font from its publisher and keeps its licence beside it in the project.
- **Noto emoji** are fetched by `scripts/fetch_noto_emoji.py` into each project: animated ones from [Noto Emoji Animation](https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-animation/), which asks for a credit line ("Animated emoji: Google Noto") in the film's credits or notes; static fallbacks are Apache-2.0.
- **Fusion Pixel Font** (`pixel-breakdown`). `assets/fonts.json` gives the release URL, size and sha256 of the 12 px proportional BDF archive by [TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font); `scripts/setup_project.py` downloads it on first use into `~/.cache/pixel-breakdown/`, checks it, and copies the BDF for the episode's language together with `OFL.txt` and the `LICENSES/` folder of the upstream pixel fonts into the project. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **vibe-knowledge fonts.** `skills/vibe-knowledge/assets/fonts.json` pins Geist and Geist Mono ([vercel/geist-font](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font)), Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC ([google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts)) and LXGW WenKai ([lxgw/LxgwWenKai](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai)) by commit or release with their sha256; `scripts/fonts.py` downloads them on first use into `~/.cache/vibe-knowledge/fonts/` and copies them with their `OFL.txt` into the workspace. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **talking-head-editor fonts.** `skills/talking-head-editor/assets/fonts/fonts.json` pins 27 fonts (Source Han Sans and Serif from Adobe's releases, Smiley Sans, Douyin Sans from bytedance/fonts, and Google Fonts families such as Anton, Oswald, Montserrat, ZCOOL KuaiLe and Ma Shan Zheng) by commit or release with their sha256; `scripts/fonts.py` downloads them on first use into `~/.cache/koubo/fonts/` with their licences from `assets/fonts/licenses/`. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **talking-head-editor sound and music.** `skills/talking-head-editor/scripts/setup_earshot.py install` clones a pinned commit of [earshot](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot) (MIT) into its own environment on request. Mixkit sound effects and music, and Incompetech music (CC BY 4.0, credit required), are downloaded when picked; nothing is committed.
- **talking-head-editor cut-out model.** `scripts/person_matte.py` downloads `rvm_mobilenetv3_fp32.onnx` from the [Robust Video Matting](https://github.com/PeterL1n/RobustVideoMatting) release (GPL-3.0) on first use into `~/.cache/koubo-matte/`; it is run with ONNX Runtime and never shipped. 3D icon stickers come from [Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji) (MIT) on use; JianYing drafts are written with [pyJianYingDraft](https://github.com/GuanYixuan/pyJianYingDraft) (Apache-2.0), installed by the user.
- **earshot** ([Leonxlnx/earshot](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot), MIT): `skills/vibe-knowledge/scripts/earshot.py install` clones a pinned commit into its own virtual environment on request; its sound recipes render code-made sound effects.
- **vibe-knowledge methods.** `skills/vibe-knowledge/references/11-absorbed.md` lists the open-source explainer projects whose methods were studied and which licence each carries; the skill's code and text are its own, and projects without a permissive licence contributed ideas only.
